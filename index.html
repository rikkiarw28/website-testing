Dulu senang menggambar, tapi akhirnya memilih jalan yang berbeda — masuk ke dunia teknik, dan sekarang menekuni industri farmasi.

Beberapa tahun terakhir diwarnai banyak overthinking soal pendidikan, pekerjaan, hubungan, dan hal-hal lain yang bikin pusing. Ternyata benar kata orang, jadi dewasa itu menyenangkan sekaligus tidak mudah dijalani.

Jalan ke depan mungkin tidak terlihat mulus, tapi tidak apa-apa. Masa depan tetap cerah, dan saya tidak sabar melihat ke mana semua ini akan bermuara. Bismillah.

The Story

I believe that life holds special magic for those who dare to dream. Unfortunately, factors outside of anyone’s control make it hard for some people to reach their potential: things like when they were born, who their parents, and where they grew up.

I wake up every day determined to use our resources to create a world where everyone has the opportunity to lead a healthy and productive life to reach their dreams. Most importantly, I believe this: All lives have equal value. That’s why I made the decision to help others.

This project isn’t fully active yet – as I am currently focused on strengthening my earning power, but it will be very soon, Insha Allah. If you are interested, please let me know. It would be great to collaborate with you.
