Building

Arwenda Foundation

I believe that life holds special magic for those who Dare to Dream. Unfortunately, factors outside of anyone's control make it hard for some people: things like when and where they were born and grew up, and who their parents. That's why I want to use my resources to help more people live healthy and productive life so they can find their potential. Because all lives have equal value.

This project isn’t fully active yet – as I am currently focused on strengthening my capital, but it will be very soon, Insha Allah.
