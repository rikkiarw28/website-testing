The Story

I believe that life holds special magic for those who Dare to Dream. Unfortunately, factors outside of anyone's control make it hard for some people: things like when and where they were born and grew up, and who their parents. That's why I want to use the resources I have to help more people live healthy and productive life to reach their potential. Because lives have equal value. That's why I made the decision to help others.

This project isn’t fully active yet – as I am currently focused on strengthening my earning power, but it will be very soon, Insha Allah. If you are interested, please let me know. It would be great to collaborate with you.
